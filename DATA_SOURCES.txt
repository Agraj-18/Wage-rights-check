WAGE & RIGHTS CHECKER  -  WHERE THE NUMBERS CAME FROM
=====================================================

None of these numbers come straight from a government gazette. They come from public
websites that summarise the notifications. CHECK EVERY NUMBER on the official site of
the state labour department before the demo, and fix min_wages.csv.

Each row of min_wages.csv has a "confidence" label. The app shows a matching warning.

  good        Tamil Nadu, listed on WageIndicator for 1 April 2026 (8 jobs).
              Still shows "may be out of date" after about 6 months.
  older       Tamil Nadu, listed on WageIndicator as valid in March 2026 (18 jobs).
              These are probably a little lower than the April 2026 figures.
  stand_in    Cracker / Fireworks Factory (Tamil Nadu). NO official fireworks rate was
              found. The Match Manufacturing rate is used as a stand-in. Piece-rate work
              (paid per 1000) cannot be checked exactly. Get the real rate from the
              Tamil Nadu Labour Department.
  disputed    Other states. Different websites gave DIFFERENT monthly figures
              (for example Kerala unskilled: 13,500 to 18,200). The app keeps the lowest
              and highest figure and compares the worker's pay with both.
  indicative  Other states where only one website gave a figure.

Other states (monthly, "main / big-city zone", divided by 26 to get a day):
  Figures were collected from public summary websites in October 2026, including
  compliance blogs and payroll sites. They often disagree and many have no clear date.
  The states are: Uttar Pradesh, Bihar, Maharashtra, West Bengal, Rajasthan, Madhya
  Pradesh, Gujarat, Karnataka, Andhra Pradesh, Telangana, Kerala, Odisha, Jharkhand,
  Delhi, Haryana, Punjab.

Not included on purpose
  - Tamil Nadu shops & establishments by zone (A-D): the sources disagreed.
  - Zone-wise rates for other states (only the main zone is used).
  - Piece rates (for example beedi rolling, per 1000).

Official places to check
  - Tamil Nadu Labour Department: labour.tn.gov.in
  - Your state's labour department website
  - SAMADHAN portal: samadhan.labour.gov.in

To add or fix a rate: open min_wages.csv and edit or add a row. Keep the column names.
rate_low and rate_high are RUPEES PER DAY. If you only know a monthly figure, divide by 26.
If you know the exact rate, put the same number in rate_low and rate_high.
